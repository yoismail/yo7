Delivery Information

Where we deliver, what it costs, and what to expect.

Enter your postcode, we'll tell you straight away.

Currently delivering across

Postcode areas: CO, IP, CM, and parts of SS & CB. Not seeing your town? Message us, we're expanding steadily.

Delivery area

Essex & Suffolk only, for now. The radius grows as we do.

£

Delivery fee

A flat £3.50 per order, shown at checkout before you pay.

Minimum order

£15 minimum on all delivered orders. No minimum for pickup.

Delivery window

Most local orders arrive within 24 to 48 hours of confirmation.

Pickup option

Prefer to collect? Choose pickup at checkout, no delivery fee.

Delivery conditions, worth knowing

  • Orders placed before 2pm are usually delivered the same day or next day, depending on your area.
  • Someone needs to be available at the delivery address to receive the order, especially for frozen and perishable items.
  • Frozen and fresh items are packed separately to keep everything at the right temperature until it reaches your door.
  • Outside the Essex & Suffolk radius right now, but want to be first to know when that changes? Message us on WhatsApp.
  • Delivery notes (gate codes, preferred times, safe places) can be added at checkout, we read every one.
  • Something arrive damaged or wrong? Message us within 24 hours and we'll sort it, no hassle.
