About Us

Nigerian, West African and Caribbean groceries, sold online and ready to collect in Ipswich.

Who we are

YO7 Foods sells the African and Caribbean groceries you'd normally have to travel for: rice, garri, palm oil, fresh meat and fish, spices, drinks and snacks, all from one place online, with collection in Ipswich.

Finding African and Nigerian food online in the UK usually means digging through cluttered, outdated websites with no real stock information. YO7 Foods is built to be easier than that: a proper online shop where you can actually find what you're looking for.

Our story

Why Yo7

YO7 Foods started in Ipswich as a way to make it easier to find proper African and Caribbean groceries: rice, garri, palm oil, spices, meat and fish, plus the drinks and snacks that don't usually make it onto UK supermarket shelves.

We deliver across the UK and offer local collection in Ipswich, serving home cooks, students and anyone who wants these ingredients without a long drive to find them.

We serve African and Caribbean communities across the UK, with strong roots in Suffolk and Essex, as well as anyone who's new to this food and wants to try it.

Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what we do with it.

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

YO7 Foods Limited ("Yo7", "we", "us"), 7 Lancaster Road, Ipswich, IP4 2NY. You can reach us at hello@yo7foods.co.uk or via WhatsApp.

What we collect

When you place an order, we ask for your name, phone number, and (for delivery orders) your address and postcode. If you sign up for an account, we ask for your name and email. If you sign up to our newsletter, we store the email address you give us so we can send you updates, you can unsubscribe at any time.

We don't set our own tracking or advertising cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall site traffic, this is a privacy-friendly, cookie-free service that doesn't track you individually or store any personal data in your browser. The site does store your shopping basket and your dark/light mode preference in your browser's local storage, so they're not lost between visits; discount codes are checked against our own records when applied, not tracked in your browser. If you pay with a method that sends you to another site and back (Klarna, Revolut Pay, Amazon Pay), your order details are held very briefly in session storage to survive that round trip, this clears itself when you close the tab and we delete it ourselves once your order is placed. When you pay, our payment processor Stripe sets a small number of its own strictly-necessary cookies (for fraud prevention and to keep your payment session secure); these are set and controlled by Stripe, not us, and are exempt from cookie consent requirements because they're essential to processing your payment.

Payment information

Card and every other payment method we offer, including Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna, Revolut Pay, Amazon Pay, PayPal, Clearpay, and Pay by Bank, are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details, they're sent directly to Stripe over an encrypted connection. Stripe is a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor.

Job applications

If you apply for a role on our Careers page, we collect your name, email, and whatever else you choose to include, phone number, a message, and a CV if you attach one. This is used only to consider you for that role and get in touch about it, it isn't added to our marketing list or used for anything else. Applications are visible only to YO7 Foods admin staff reviewing that role, and are kept until the role is filled or closed, at which point they're deleted along with the listing, CV file included.

What we do with it

  • Order details (name, address, phone) are used to fulfil and deliver your order, nothing more.
  • We don't sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Where we need to share delivery details with a courier to get your order to you, we only share what's necessary for that delivery.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@yo7foods.co.uk. We'll respond as quickly as we reasonably can.

Changes to this policy

If how we handle your information changes, particularly once real payment processing goes live, we'll update this page and change the date above.

Returns & Refunds Policy

What to do if something's wrong with your order, and how refunds work.

Last updated: August 2026

Food and perishable items

Because most of what we sell is food, we're generally not able to accept returns once an order has been delivered, for hygiene and food safety reasons. This is standard practice across food retailers.

If something arrives damaged, wrong, or faulty

If an item arrives damaged, isn't what you ordered, or there's a genuine problem with it, contact us within 48 hours of delivery on WhatsApp or at hello@yo7foods.co.uk with a photo where possible, and we'll sort out a replacement or refund for that item.

Non-food items

For non-food items (such as hair or body/oral care products), unopened and unused items can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Contact us first so we can arrange it.

Cancelling before delivery

If you need to cancel or change an order, contact us as soon as possible, if it hasn't already been prepared or dispatched, we'll cancel it and refund you in full.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Once agreed, we aim to process it within 3 to 5 working days, though your bank or card provider may take a little longer to show it. Refunds for a single damaged or wrong item are for that item, not necessarily the whole order, unless the whole order was affected. For cancellations before dispatch, the full order amount is refunded.

What we need from you

To process a refund quickly, please get in touch as soon as you notice a problem, include your order number, and a photo if the issue is visible (damaged packaging, wrong item, etc). This isn't a hard requirement, but it helps us sort things out faster.

Delivery Policy

The terms behind how we get your order to you. For weight-based pricing and the postcode checker, see Delivery Information.

Last updated: August 2026

Where we deliver

We'll deliver to every UK postcode. Delivery is priced by the total weight of your order, not a flat rate, you can see the exact fee before you check out. Pickup from our Ipswich address will always be free.

Delivery timing

Orders placed before 10am on a working day will usually be out the same or next working day, and orders placed after 10am will be treated as next-working-day at the earliest, this gives us time to actually prepare the order properly rather than rush it. We don't dispatch on weekends: an order placed after Friday's cutoff, or any time over the weekend, goes out the following Monday. Timings will be estimates, not guarantees, actual delivery is carried out by a third-party courier and can be affected by things outside our control (weather, courier delays, and so on).

If you're not in when we deliver

You can leave delivery notes at checkout (a gate code, a safe place, a preferred time) and the courier will follow them where possible. If nobody's available and there's no safe place to leave the order, the courier will typically attempt redelivery or leave a card with next steps. For perishable items, please try to make sure someone can take the delivery in reasonably promptly.

Wrong or incomplete address

Please double-check your delivery address and postcode at checkout. If an order is delayed or misdelivered because the address given was incomplete or incorrect, we'll do what we reasonably can to help sort it out, but we can't guarantee a free redelivery in that situation.

Substitutions

At checkout you can choose whether we're allowed to substitute an out-of-stock item with a reasonably similar one, or leave it out of your order entirely. Either way, you're only charged for what's actually delivered.

Risk and ownership

Risk in your order (loss or damage) passes to you once it's delivered to the address you provided, or collected at pickup. If something arrives damaged, see our Returns & Refunds Policy.

Cookie Policy

What we actually use, in plain terms, not a generic template.

Last updated: September 2026

The short version

We don't use cookies or similar technology for analytics, social media, or advertising, there's nothing on this site tracking you across the web or building an ad profile. Everything below exists to make the site actually work.

What we use, and why

  • Payment (Stripe): when you pay, our payment processor Stripe sets a small number of its own cookies for fraud prevention and to keep your payment session secure. These are set and controlled by Stripe, not us, and are strictly necessary to processing your payment, there's no way to take payment online without them.
  • Site analytics (Cloudflare): we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall traffic (how many people visit, which pages). It's a genuinely cookie-free, privacy-friendly service, it doesn't track you individually, doesn't use a persistent identifier, and doesn't store anything in your browser.
  • Local storage, not cookies: the site stores a small amount of information directly in your browser (not as a cookie) to make it work properly: your shopping basket and your dark/light mode preference. This stays on your device, persists between visits so your basket isn't lost, and is never sent anywhere. Discount code usage isn't tracked this way, it's checked against our own records when you apply a code, not something stored in your browser.
  • Session storage, for one payment round-trip only: if you pay with a method that sends you to another site and back (Klarna, Revolut Pay, Amazon Pay), your order details (delivery address, phone, basket) are held very briefly in your browser's session storage so nothing is lost on the way back. Unlike the local storage above, this clears itself the moment you close the tab, and we delete it ourselves as soon as your order is placed.

Why there's no "accept/reject" banner

UK rules on cookies (PECR) only require asking your permission for non-essential ones, things like advertising trackers or third-party analytics that profile you. Since everything above is either strictly necessary to take your payment or doesn't use cookies at all, there's nothing optional here to ask permission for. If that changes, if we ever add analytics or advertising that isn't cookie-free, we'll add a proper consent banner before it goes live and update this page first.

Controlling cookies yourself

Since the only cookies here are Stripe's, and they're required to complete a payment, blocking them will stop checkout from working. You can still control cookies generally through your browser's settings if you'd like to see what's stored.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, email us at hello@yo7foods.co.uk. See also our Privacy Policy for how we handle your personal information more broadly.
